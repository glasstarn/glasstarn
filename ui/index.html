    Starting a new project?

    Start with a question in your agent.

    Type /glasstarn in your agent, or just ask. For example: “Why are our invoices taking longer to process?” or “Are invoices approved before payment?”

    1. Bring your dataYour agent asks for extracts and context, then confirms the mapping with you.
    2. Get findingsglasstarn reveals how your work really flows, straight from your data. Your agent explains the evidence and saves the project.
    3. See it for yourselfExplore the process map and the evidence behind every finding in glasstarn. Keep asking questions in the same agent conversation.

        Common-object timing
        Time between steps
        
                
        Measures only through objects that participate in both activities. Results are elapsed time, not waiting time.
        
                
        
                  
                  
                  
                  
                
        
                
        
              
        
              
        
                
        Multi-object timing
        See how object histories converge
        
                
        Separates the spread between earlier object events from elapsed time after the latest prior event. These are observable elapsed times, not inferred waiting or availability.
        
                
        
                  
                  
                
        
                
        
              
        
              
        
                
        Timing priorities
        Rank object-centric bottlenecks
        
                
        Ranks total observed timing burden. Lifecycle elapsed and multi-object convergence spread stay separate; rank is not a causal diagnosis.
